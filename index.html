Self-care & escalation

Care guide

Mission-approved instructions take priority. Use these prompts to know what to review and what to communicate.

Need urgent help?

For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, follow your onboard emergency medical protocol now and alert the crew. Do not wait for a trend chart or a message from Earth.

Emergency protocol

Heart & circulation

Record assigned vitals and any palpitations, dizziness, swelling, chest discomfort, or shortness of breath. Spaceflight fluid shifts can affect cardiovascular function.

Sleep & behavioral health

Track sleep opportunity, mood, stress, and crew connection. Note persistent changes and use the private behavioral health support available on your mission.

Bone & muscle

Log your mission-prescribed exercise and new pain, weakness, or movement difficulty. Follow your individualized exercise and nutrition plan.

Immune & habitat

Record feverish feelings, rashes, signs of infection, or unusual exposure. Follow onboard hygiene, environmental monitoring, and medical reporting procedures.

Escalate now when symptoms are severe, sudden, or rapidly worseningExamples include severe chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, fainting, new confusion, seizure, uncontrolled bleeding, or a sudden major change in vision or strength. Follow the mission emergency checklist and contact the crew/medical support immediately. This list does not replace your mission protocol.
